From Garment Intake to Handover: Our 10-Step Standard

100% Quality Inspected
1

Forensic Inspection

Fabric fiber stress analysis, color fastness test, missing buttons & pocket check.

2

Digital Barcode Tagging

Individual waterproof barcode tag assigned. Zero chance of garment mix-ups.

3

Ultrasonic Spotting

Targeted pre-treatment of wine, oil, ink, makeup & collar grime using enzyme guns.

4

RO Soft-Water Soaking

Mineral-free dematerialized water bath with optical brighteners and fabric conditioners.

5

Precision Pre-Brushing

Gentle manual pre-brushing of cuffs, collars, and trouser hems prior to washing.

6

Microprocessor Drum Wash

Variable RPM soft-drum rotation with customized temperature control per fabric.

7

6-Bar Italian Steam Press

Vacuum-suction ironing table with dry steam to sculpt sharp creases without shine.

8

Protective Packaging

Individually packed in breathable, dust-resistant transparent garment covers.

9

Master Quality Approval

Multi-angle lightbox inspection by senior QC supervisor before delivery clearance.

10

White-Glove Delivery

Punctual doorstep handover directly to your home, hostel gate, or hotel reception.
