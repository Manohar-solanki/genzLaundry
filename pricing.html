COMMON QUESTIONS

Pricing & Pickup FAQs

Clear answers so you never have to wonder about costs, turnaround, or payment.

Is doorstep pickup and delivery really free across Jodhpur?
Yes! We provide complimentary doorstep pickup and delivery for orders above ₹199 across all prominent areas of Jodhpur, including Ratanada, Shastri Nagar, Sardarpura, Paota, Chopasni, Mandore, Old Blue City, and AIIMS area.
How does Express 4 to 6-Hour delivery work?
When booking via WhatsApp or phone, mention that you need Express Turnaround. Our express runner collects your clothes, tags them with priority flags, and our facility fast-tracks wash, tumble dry, and steam pressing. A modest express surcharge applies based on order volume.
How do you protect delicate silk sarees and designer lehengas?
Delicate silk, chiffon, hand-embroidery, and zardozi bridal wear undergo 100% waterless hydrocarbon dry cleaning. Each item is pre-treated with ultrasonic stain lifters and finished with gentle form reshaping. It is returned inside a sealed, breathable luxury garment cover.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all modern payment methods: Cash on Delivery, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Credit/Debit Cards, and Bank Transfer for commercial accounts. You only pay after your clothes are inspected and returned fresh!
Are there special discounts for students or bulk orders?
Yes! Students from MBM University, AIIMS, NLU, and IIT Jodhpur receive an instant flat 15% discount by displaying their valid college ID. Bulk orders above 30 pieces or hotel commercial linens receive custom tiered volume pricing.
